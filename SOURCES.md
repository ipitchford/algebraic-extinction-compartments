# Sources and exact target

The originating mathematical statement is Chan, Crommelin and Mandjes,
*Asymptotic behavior for a general class of spreading models*,
arXiv:2511.02437v1, Conjecture 1, §3.3:
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02437v1 .

The model coefficients are defined in §2.2. Transition and dependency graphs are
Definitions 6 and 7. Assumption 1 is transition acyclicity. Assumption 2 requires
each dependency cycle to contain a state with a persistent dependency successor;
that successor need not lie outside the cycle. Theorem 2 gives integrability,
which the present example preserves.

The source's displayed document date and bibliographic submission year differ.
This release pins the inspected version and statement rather than inferring a
revision chronology. A later changed conjecture would need a separate comparison.

All other references and precise scope comparisons are in `paper.md` and
`CITATION_AUDIT.md`. No third-party full-text publication is redistributed.
